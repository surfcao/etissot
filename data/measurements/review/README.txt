Declared checkpoint audit inputs, retained for the October 2026 manuscript review.

csp_precision.json contains site-level fit/Jacobian measurements and selected radius sweeps; summary quantiles and the manuscript table are regenerated from the rows. review_followups.json contains retained aggregate checkpoint-evaluation results for activation crossings, bearing-count sensitivity and replay; these aggregates are declared inputs, not fresh model evaluations. The common-radius profiles are separately retained in data/profiles/common_radius_0p3.npz and are refitted by cached reproduction. Original collection commands are scripts/review_followups.py and scripts/csp_precision_check.py; model files and CSP source must be obtained separately. Upstream rights and evaluation-precision qualifications continue to apply.

radius_screening.json contains increment curves (directional stretch on 32-bearing rings at 34 radii from 1 m to 30 km) for the thirteen pinned checkpoints at four fixed-grid sites, with the maximum relative difference from the cached profiles at each primary radius. It was collected with scripts/radius_screening.py; cached reproduction (etissot.reporting.radius_screening) derives the ratios quoted in the supplement and plots radius_screening.png.
