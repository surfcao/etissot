The bounded demonstration selects sites before refinement using cached fields: two nearest the median anisotropy within $60^\circ$, two with the largest anisotropy through $80^\circ$, and up to two initial fallback sites. Selection uses Jacobian anisotropy where available, otherwise finite-lag anisotropy. Two further sites, $(0^\circ,179.999^\circ)$ and $(45^\circ,179.999^\circ)$, test refinement within a narrow chart clearance. Categories may overlap; the sample is intentionally difficult and is not an estimate of global success probability.

The start is the checkpoint's main audit radius, reduced first if needed to keep the disc inside the chart. Up to 16 additional halvings require two consecutive minor-eigenvalue-relative tensor changes at most 0.005, with no fallback. Every sampled profile, fitted tensor, radius, chart reduction and termination is stored. The final radius of an unconverged run is not accepted as a valid differential estimate.
