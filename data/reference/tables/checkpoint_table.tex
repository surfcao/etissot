GeoCLIP is the bundled location encoder in package 1.2.1, file \texttt{location\_encoder\_weights.pth}. SatCLIP filenames follow \texttt{satclip-}, followed by backbone (vit16, resnet18 or resnet50) and degree (l10 or l40), with extension \texttt{.ckpt}. SINR files have prefix \texttt{model\_an\_full\_}\allowbreak\texttt{input\_enc\_}\allowbreak\texttt{sin\_cos\_}, with cap-10, cap-1000 or environment-distilled suffixes. CSP uses the iNat2018 and fMoW \texttt{UNSUPER} grid-cell checkpoints, not the subsequent supervised variants. Climplicit uses \texttt{climplicit-3341daa2.pth}, with a direct float64 \texttt{location\_encoder} path and explicit month features. Longitudes are wrapped to $[-180^\circ,180^\circ)$ before encoding; raw pole/seam equivalence is assessed separately. The metadata records complete filenames, dimensions, normalisation, precision and full hashes in one machine-readable table.
